Libraries are no longer only about books. Vestbro was conceived as the living room of a dense neighbourhood: a place to read, work, rehearse, wait out the rain. The challenge was to give a deep urban block the generosity of daylight usually reserved for buildings in the open.

Our answer is a single void. A square light well, eleven metres across, cuts through all five floors and ends in a skylight tuned to the angle of the winter sun. Reading rooms ring the void, stepping back as they rise, so each floor receives light without glare.

The shelving, stairs and handrails are built from one species of Danish beech. Over time, hands will darken the timber exactly where people linger most.

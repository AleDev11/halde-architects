Kystlys means *coastal light*. The brief was simple: a house for a family of five that feels as calm in January as it does in June. We began not with a plan but with a year of sun studies on the site, tracing how light crosses the dune from the first grey of morning to the long, low evenings of the Nordic summer.

The result is a sequence of four concrete volumes, each turned a few degrees toward its hour. Breakfast happens in the east room, where the sun arrives at 6:40 in midsummer. The studio faces north for steady, shadowless light. The living room catches the last of the day across the water.

Materials are few and honest: board-marked concrete, untreated oak, lime plaster. Nothing is applied that the weather will need to argue with.

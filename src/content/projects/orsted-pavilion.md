The pavilion was designed to be dismantled. Every member is the same length of Danish spruce, notched at both ends and held by a single steel pin. Assembled in a rotating pattern, the identical parts produce a canopy that seems to branch and thin like a stand of trees.

Through the day the roof throws a moving lattice of shadow onto the lawn below. At noon the pattern is tight and dark; by evening it stretches across the grass in long diagonal bars. Visitors began to use it as a clock.

When the season ended, the pieces were numbered, packed and rebuilt as an outdoor classroom for a school in Valby.
